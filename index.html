This is our prototype that classifys Flint and Arrowheads.

Our Goals:

Our Goal for this project is to create an image recognition AI that can help educated archaeologist or archaeology hobbyists identify the difference between similar looking flint and arrowheads or other artifacts. We hope that this product will eventually be usable by archaeologists in the field and help them correctly document unknown artifacts.
